# SoloCoco copy revision

Copy approved by the owner on 2026-10-28, following an interview about the voice and scope of this revision. This document records the wording used by the application and artwork.

## Agreed direction

Use conversational Brazilian Portuguese with ordinary sentences and occasional dry humour. Write for someone who wants something cold and tasty during an ordinary day. Lead with flavour and refreshment. Avoid repeated punchlines, symmetrical fragments, forced slang and invented founder or origin stories. Beach references can appear occasionally.

The owner's reference is: "Coco com um pouco de gás. Basta abrir a lata e tomar gelado."

Keep SoloCoco, the tagline "Só o coco.", the six Flavour names, product details, prices, nutrition and visual design. Include can-label wording and the sharing preview in the review. Functional labels and fictional-project disclosures stay clear.
