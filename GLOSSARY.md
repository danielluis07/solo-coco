# Glossary

**SoloCoco**: The fictional Brazilian brand of lightly sparkling coconut drink the landing page promotes. The name plays on *só o coco* ("just coconut").

**Flavour**: One product variant in the SoloCoco lineup, sold as its own can: the coconut base plus one Brazilian fruit.

**Copy**: Any text a visitor reads on the landing page. Always Brazilian Portuguese.

**Quick-add**: The per-Flavour pack and price picker on the landing page. Decorative only; nothing is ever bought.
_Avoid_: cart, checkout
