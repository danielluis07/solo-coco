# Glossary

**SoloCoco**: The fictional Brazilian brand of lightly sparkling coconut drink the landing page promotes. The name plays on *só o coco* ("just coconut").

**Flavour**: One product variant in the SoloCoco lineup, sold as its own can: the coconut base plus one Brazilian fruit, except the plain coconut Flavour, which has no fruit. The lineup is six Flavours: the plain one and five fruit ones.

**Copy**: Any text a visitor reads on the landing page. Always Brazilian Portuguese.

**Quick-add**: The per-Flavour pack and price picker on the landing page. Decorative only; nothing is ever bought.
_Avoid_: cart, checkout

**Hero**: The opening section of the landing page, normally pinned while the coconut becomes the can as the visitor scrolls. Its static fallback presents a poster and Copy in normal page flow.

**Beat**: One named step of the Hero's scroll choreography (Girar, Facão, Selos, Fechar, Virar lata, Lata gira, Saída). Listed in `DESIGN.md` § Hero choreography.
_Avoid_: scene, step
